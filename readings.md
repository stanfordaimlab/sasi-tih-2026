# Readings

Every reading gets a **card**: why you're reading it, one question to read for, and
where to find it. Your written response goes in the workbook, not here.

> **For editors (Jason/Dara) — copyright rules, non-negotiable:**
> 1. Never upload or commit a paywalled paper, book chapter, or industry report. Link out.
> 2. Open-access is embeddable **only** after checking the license on that specific
>    paper (look for CC-BY on the paper itself, not the journal homepage).
> 3. Prefer an open-access reading over a paywalled one when two teach the same lesson.
> 4. When the ideal source is locked, Dara writes a 300–500 word original explainer instead.
