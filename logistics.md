# How this program works

## Anatomy of a session

Every Saturday session follows the same shape:

| Time (PT) | Block |
|---|---|
| 12:00–12:10 | Welcome & orientation |
| 12:10–12:55 | Segment 1 |
| ~1:00 | Break (10 min) |
| 1:05–1:50 | Segment 2 |
| 1:50–2:00 | Wrap-up & preview |

## Between sessions

Each session has short assigned readings with guiding questions — see
[Readings](readings.html) — and a post-session survey via email from Qualtrics!
**Completing every survey is required for your certificate.**


## If you have to miss a session

Email summermed@stanford.edu ahead of time, then use this hub and the session page to
catch up on readings and homework.
