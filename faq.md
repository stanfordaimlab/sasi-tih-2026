# FAQ

**Is this program virtual or in-person?**
Entirely virtual, live on Zoom. Zoom links come via Slack and your calendar invite.

**Do I need coding, healthcare, or AI experience?**
No. Bring an open mind and your own perspective.

**How much time each week?**
The two-hour Saturday session plus roughly 1–2 hours of readings, homework, and the
survey. The optional lab adds one hour.

**Will there be homework?**
Yes — short readings with guiding questions and a brief assignment. Details on each
session page and in Slack.

**How do I interact with speakers?**
Every guest session includes live Q&A. Questions you didn't get to ask can be relayed
by the teaching team afterward — post them in Slack.

**Will I receive a certificate?**
Yes, on completion of the program — you must complete **every weekly survey** to
receive it.

**Can I miss a session?**
Email summermed@stanford.edu in advance, then use this hub to catch up.
